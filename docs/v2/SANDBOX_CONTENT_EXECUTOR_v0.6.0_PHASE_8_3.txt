LIFE.SAVER V2 Phase 8.3 — Sandbox Content Executor
====================================================

Purpose:
Test a content_publish executor shape without touching external social platforms.

Implemented executor:
sandboxContentExecutor

Action type:
content_publish

Required executor methods:
- validate(context)
- execute(context)
- rollback(context, result)
- summarizeResult(result)

execute() returns sandbox-only fake data:
- fake_external_post_id
- fake_permalink
- sandbox_success

Safety boundary:
This executor is sandbox-only. It does not publish to Instagram, Meta, TikTok, LinkedIn, Shopify, email, ads, or any external API.

The fake permalink uses:
https://sandbox.lifesaveragent.com/...

This is a simulated permalink only, not proof of a published post.

Action-flow status:
- Not wired to auto-run.
- Not wired to approval queue execution endpoint.
- Not connected to external tokens.
- Not connected to social APIs.
- Not a real publishing path.

Future phases may connect sandbox handlers to a controlled action runner. Real executors must not be added until the roadmap explicitly reaches the relevant phase and all approval, pause, policy, caps, idempotency, audit, and rollback checks are enforced.
