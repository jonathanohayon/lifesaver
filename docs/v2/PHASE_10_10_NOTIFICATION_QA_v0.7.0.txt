LIFE.SAVER v0.7.0 — PHASE 10.10 NOTIFICATION QA
================================================

Purpose:
Finalize Phase 10 by producing a safe notification QA report.

Scope:
- Verify in-app notification preview.
- Verify email notification template preview.
- Verify approval deep link opens the exact action review screen.
- Verify auth-required behavior remains required.
- Verify failed notification delivery logs are safe and present.

Deliverable:
Notification QA report.

Endpoints:
- GET /api/v1/notifications/qa/status
- GET /api/v1/notifications/qa/report

Report checks:
1. in_app_notification
   Confirms the notification center can show one pending approval and a safe review link.

2. email_notification
   Confirms the approval email template includes action title, action type, risk level, reason, and review link.
   It also confirms no email is sent in this phase.

3. deep_link
   Confirms the approval link opens actions.html with the exact actionId and linkMode=review_only.

4. auth_required
   Confirms the link requires login and cannot approve from the email click alone.

5. failed_notification_logs
   Confirms failed notification delivery logs can represent notification_failed safely.

Security notes:
- QA endpoints are mounted behind authRequired.
- QA report is read-only.
- No external provider is called.
- No email is sent.
- No Slack message is sent.
- No action state is mutated.
- No LinkedIn publishing behavior is changed.
- No raw payload_json is exposed.
- No rollback payload is exposed.
- No OAuth tokens, SMTP secrets, Slack secrets, Triple Whale keys, Claude keys, database URLs, or worker secrets are exposed.
