Risk scoring behavior:
- The score is deterministic.
- Total score is capped at 100.
- Risk levels: low, medium, high, critical.
- Critical or very high scores are blocked by risk score.
- Lower scores still remain manual-review only until a later phase explicitly adds auto-run gates.

Important safety boundary:
This phase does not enable auto-run.
It only prepares one required safety signal for a later narrow content auto-run lane.
