LIFE.SAVER v0.7.0 — Phase 13.5 FAQ Auto-Reply Policy
=====================================================

Purpose:
Add the policy evaluator for the future safe FAQ auto-reply lane.

Roadmap gates implemented:
- FAQ category matches.
- Confidence is high.
- Ticket is low risk.
- Cap is not exceeded.
- A rule explicitly allows auto-reply.

Extra safety gates added:
- One recipient only.
- Phase 13.4 thread association must be verified.
- Sensitive/escalation tickets are not eligible.

Important safety boundary:
This phase does not enable auto-send. It only evaluates whether a support_reply_send action would be eligible for a future FAQ auto-reply lane. Current package behavior remains manual approval first for real sends.

Endpoints:
- GET  /api/v1/support/faq-auto-reply-policy/status
- GET  /api/v1/support/faq-auto-reply-policy/example
- POST /api/v1/support/faq-auto-reply-policy/preview
