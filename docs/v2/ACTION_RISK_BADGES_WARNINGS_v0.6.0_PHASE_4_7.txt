LIFE.SAVER V2 — PHASE 4.7
Action Risk Badges + Warnings
Version: v0.6.0

Purpose
-------
Give the founder a clear visual risk signal before approving or rejecting an action.

Risk levels
-----------
1. Low
   - Routine internal proposal.
   - Still requires founder review.

2. Medium
   - Requires careful review of brand fit, timing, customer context, and source data.

3. High
   - High-impact business action.
   - Review payload, platform, and expected effect carefully.

4. Critical
   - Founder-level scrutiny required.
   - Never auto-run without explicit policy, hard caps, pause switch, audit logging, and executor safeguards.

Critical warning triggers
-------------------------
The UI highlights critical warnings for:
- Ad spend changes.
- Bulk support sends.
- Refund-related replies.
- Unusual or sensitive customer complaints.

Implementation
--------------
Phase 4.7 adds risk badge UI to:
- Action cards.
- Action detail drawer.
- Approve confirmation modal.
- Reject-with-reason modal.
- Risk guide legend on actions.html.

Safety boundary
---------------
This phase is UI-only.
It does not add executors.
It does not publish content.
It does not send support replies.
It does not change ad spend.
It does not pause campaigns.
It does not write to external platforms.

Approval and rejection still only record internal LIFE.SAVER status changes through existing Phase 3 endpoints.

Testing
-------
1. Run db:seed:actions:local.
2. Open actions.html.
3. Confirm risk badges appear on action cards.
4. Confirm high/critical warnings appear where applicable.
5. Open action detail drawer.
6. Confirm risk explanation includes risk level, critical triggers, and review rule.
7. Open approve modal.
8. Confirm risk warning appears before final approval.
9. Open reject modal.
10. Confirm risk warning appears before final rejection.
11. Confirm no external write happens.
