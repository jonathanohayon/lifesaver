LIFE.SAVER UI — ACTION RISK BADGES + WARNINGS
Version: v0.6.0 Phase 4.7

UI placement
------------
- Risk guide legend at the top of actions.html.
- Risk badge in each action card header.
- Risk alert panel inside high/critical action cards.
- Risk explanation panel in the action detail drawer.
- Risk warning boxes inside approve and reject modals.

Risk visual language
--------------------
Low:
- Green tone.
- Routine review.

Medium:
- Amber tone.
- Check brand/data/timing.

High:
- Orange tone.
- Business-impact warning.

Critical:
- Red tone.
- Founder-level scrutiny.

Critical action examples
------------------------
- ad_budget_adjust
- ad_pause
- bulk support replies
- refund-related support replies
- unusual/sensitive complaints

Safety note
-----------
Risk badges guide founder review only. They do not approve, reject, execute, publish, send, spend, pause, refund, or write externally.
