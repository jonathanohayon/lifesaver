LIFE.SAVER UI — Action Status Filters
Version: v0.6.0 Phase 4.4

Placement:
- actions.html, directly above the advanced filter toolbar.

Component behavior:
- Status tabs act as quick filters.
- Advanced status dropdown remains available.
- Tabs and dropdown stay synchronized.
- Filter changes reload GET /api/v1/actions with status query parameter.
- Empty states remain safe and clear.

Primary tabs:
- All
- Proposed
- Approved
- Rejected
- Executed
- Failed
- Cancelled
- Rolled Back

Safety:
- Filter controls are read-only.
- They do not approve/reject/cancel actions.
- They do not execute anything.
- They do not expose secrets or full payload_json.
